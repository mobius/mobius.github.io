浏览器多人街机房:房主在本地加载 ROM(不上传),WebRTC 串流画面给玩家;P2–P4 选座发送输入。

来自 README:

Browser-based multiplayer arcade rooms. - Host loads a ROM locally in the browser (ROM is not uploaded). - Host streams video to other players via WebRTC. - Players pick seats (P2–P4) and send inputs back to the host via WebRTC DataChannel.
